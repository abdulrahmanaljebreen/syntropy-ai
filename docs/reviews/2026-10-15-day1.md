## Contrast: decided 19 Oct 2026
Decision: brand green is used as built, even where it fails. The primary Button (white on `bg/brand/primary`), filled brand badges, and brand green amounts, selected tiles, links and selected borders stay exactly as in Figma (the investment calculator is the reference). Skills, lint and the contrast table now treat these pairs as accepted (`brand-as-built`), not findings. The remaining items below (solid success and warning chips, `border/strong`, yellow and warning in Dark) are still open.

Original finding: White text on brand green fails WCAG AA for normal-size text: `text/on-brand` on `bg/brand/primary` is 2.92:1 in Light and 2.14:1 in Dark (needs 4.5:1). That is the primary Button label. Also failing: `text/on-brand` on `bg/success` and `bg/warning` (solid StatusPill), `text/brand/primary` on neutral surfaces in Light (2.92:1, used for links), `border/strong` in Light (2.01:1, needs 3:1 for control edges), and `text/default` on `bg/brand/yellow` / `bg/warning` in Dark (1.02:1 / 1.67:1). Full table: `knowledge/ux/accessibility/contrast-pairs.md`. Options: darken the button fill to `LightGreen/700` (#387c3f), use dark text on brand green, or accept AA-large only with a bigger bolder label. Until decided, agents are told to flag it, not to invent a raw colour.

## Findings from day 2 (doc pages and Notion)
10. **Doc pages contradict the Western-digits rule**: the Tag guide says "+N" uses Arabic-Indic numerals in RTL, the Stat guide leaves digits to locale, and the ChartTooltip RTL image shows ٢٠٢٥. Agents follow the Western-digits rule.
11. **ProgressBar guide** says there is no indeterminate variant, yet `ProgressBar_Indeterminate` exists.
12. **Investor IssuanceStatusBadge** reference labels differ from the Status option names (image shows "Scheduled" for Reserved, "Confirmed" for Raising).
13. **Currency defaults** in some components put "SAR" before the amount (`SAR 1,200`, `Minimum investment: SAR 1,000`); the rule is after the amount. One default shows `16.00%`; the rule is one decimal for percentages.
14. **Date Calendar Picker** has no Hijri option, so rule R5 (Hijri with Gregorian) cannot be met yet.
15. **Notion vs built system**: Notion still describes kebab-case tokens, a 12-40 type scale, 1.3 heading line height, Noto Kufi for Arabic, 1.5px icon strokes, Founding Day themes and WCAG 2.1. The repo states the built values and notes the old ones.
16. **Glossary**: please check the Arabic for Portfolio, Absher, Hijri/Gregorian, DGA, Ijara, Zakah and Distributions (written by us, not found in sources). The DS glossary also mixes up SDAIA and DGA.
17. **Calculator v2 rate colour**: the doc says the annual rate stays neutral, but the Duration option images show the selected term's rate in green. Please confirm the rule.
18. **Prop name typos in Figma** that agents must copy verbatim until fixed: "Has Headling", "Qualifide", "Not-adedd", "Has Lable", "Wight", "Done - Captial", and trailing spaces in "Has Multi-deals " and "Description " (Collapsed-Reservation).
19. **Investment limits (for Compliance)**: CMA crowdfunding rules cap retail clients at 25,000 SAR per issuer and 100,000 SAR per 12 months (and bar retail from asset-backed issuances), while the product rule is a 500,000 SAR cap for non-qualified investors. Which regime applies depends on Safqah's licence. Agents are told never to hardcode limits.
20. **Fee basis (for Product)**: older notes mention a flat 3,000 SAR fee incl. VAT and 2% of capital; the current rule used is 1% of profit. Please confirm, and whether VAT applies.
21. **Placeholders for Compliance**: licence number, Sharia decision reference and date, complaint handling period, and the Arabic risk lines (drafts) in `knowledge/ux/compliance/`.
22. **Focus ring**: `border/focus` resolves to neutral ink (#111312 / #edefee), while the Colors page describes a green focus ring.
23. **Radius doc drift**: `radius/default` is 20 and `radius/2xl` 24, but the Effects page text says md 8 is the default and 2xl is 20.
24. **Shadow/xs** key layer is bound to Shadow/Blur/MD while its description says Blur/XS.
25. **SafqahLogo default color**: the component guide leads with Pigment Green, the Logos page says Royal Green.
26. **No motion tokens** yet; durations in the skills are interim, taken from component docs.
27. **Missing icons**: no undo, redo or dedicated back icon (back uses arrow-right-2 / arrow-left-2).
28. **Fee label wording**: compliance files say «رسوم الإدارة (1% من الربح)», Calculator component descriptions say «رسوم الوكالة (1% من الربح)». Please pick one.
29. **Code snippets in Figma guides pass `direction: TextDirection.rtl`** (Alert, Banner, Toast, TabBar, Tooltip, EmptyState, ProgressBar), which breaks rule R1 (direction comes from the locale). Class prefixes also mix `Safqah*` and `Syntropy*`.
30. **Tab bar**: the deal-list and portfolio patterns use the 4-tab `TabBar`; the component descriptions say the shipping app uses `TabBar (3-tab)`. Please confirm which is current.
