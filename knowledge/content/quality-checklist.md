# Content quality checklist

A pass/fail checklist for any Safqah content: marketing posts, campaign copy, videos, reports, and in-product copy. Run it before publishing or before handing copy to design. The first five groups are the Notion Content Quality Checklist (written for marketing and social content); the last group applies the same standard to product UI copy.

## Rules

Each item is a yes/no check. Any "no" blocks publishing until fixed or explicitly waived by the content owner.

### 1. Audience and purpose
- [ ] Content is directed at a clearly defined segment (developer / investor / general).
- [ ] It addresses a real problem or a question the audience is actively searching for.
- [ ] It does not repeat previously published content without added value (unless repetition is essential).
Why: content without a reader and a question is noise.

### 2. Accuracy and credibility
- [ ] All figures and statistics are sourced (required when the content is, for example, a report).
- [ ] No exaggerated or unverifiable claims.
- [ ] Financial and Sharia terminology is accurate (check `glossary.md`: no "loan", "interest", "bonds", "borrower").
Why: a regulated investment platform cannot afford a wrong number or term.

### 3. Tone and copywriting
- [ ] Language is formal and platform-appropriate (see `voice-tone.md`).
- [ ] The opening line has a clear hook (for campaigns and videos).
- [ ] The call to action is specific and actionable.
Why: calm and clear, but still purposeful.

### 4. Format and platform fit
- [ ] Length suits the platform (X / Instagram / LinkedIn / Snapchat).
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant, not generic.
- [ ] The visual supports the copy and does not just repeat it.
Why: each channel has its own reading pattern.

### 5. Brand alignment
- [ ] Reflects Safqah's core values (transparency, Sharia compliance).
- [ ] No conflict with messaging from other active campaigns.
Why: one voice across channels.

### 6. Product UI copy (applies the standard to screens)
- [ ] Arabic written first; English is a translation, not the source.
- [ ] Within the length limit for its copy type (`copy-lengths.md`).
- [ ] Minimal: a label, the control, at most one short hint; no explanatory paragraphs.
- [ ] Errors give the next action (T5); success copy names the specific thing.
- [ ] Numbers follow `numbers-currency.md`: Western digits, currency after the amount, two decimals on transactions, one decimal on percentages, «نسبة» on Arabic percentage labels.
- [ ] Risk and fees stated plainly; no fake urgency, no flattery, no celebration emoji on financial events (T2, T3, T6).
- [ ] Warning states use warning wording and `Tone=warning`; red only for critical or defaulted states.
- [ ] No em dash character anywhere.
- [ ] Final copy, not placeholder, in both languages before handoff.
Why: same quality bar inside the product as outside it.

## Examples
| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | «نسبة العائد المتوقع 8.5%، تصنيف المخاطر: متوسط» with the source deal named | «عوائد تصل إلى 10%» with no source |
| CTA | «استعرض الصفقات المفتوحة» / "Browse open deals" | «اعرف أكثر» / "Learn more" on every post |
| Terminology | «صكوك متوافقة مع الشريعة» / "Sharia-compliant sukuk" | "Islamic bonds" |
| Visual fit | Chart showing the payout schedule the copy describes | A stock photo repeating the headline |
| Brand | Transparent fee breakdown | "Zero risk" claims |
