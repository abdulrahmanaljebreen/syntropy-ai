# Voice and tone

How Safqah sounds, and how the tone shifts by moment while the voice stays the same. Open this before writing any string, and again when the moment is sensitive (money leaving, a late payment, a rejection, a security event).

## The voice
Safqah is a knowledgeable advisor: **clear, calm, confident, respectful**. Not a salesperson, not a friend, not a bank's legal department. Plain Arabic that does not talk down. Exact numbers. Risk named honestly. Compliance text treated as real copy.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Clear: one idea per sentence | Clever: puns, idioms, marketing flourish |
| Calm: neutral facts, even when things go wrong | Alarmist: red, exclamation marks, «تحذير!» for non-critical states |
| Confident: short declaratives, active voice | Hedging: «قد يكون من الممكن ربما» |
| Respectful: investors are adults making financial decisions | Flattering: «أنت مستثمر ذكي» / "Smart move!" |
| Transparent: fees, risk and limits stated | Salesy: «فرصة العمر», "Don't miss out" |

## The six tone rules (T1 to T6)
| Rule | Means | Do (AR / EN) | Don't (AR / EN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Clarity over cleverness | Plain words, no buzzwords in functional copy | «صفقة جديدة متاحة» / "New deal available" | «فرصة العمر بانتظارك» / "The opportunity of a lifetime awaits" |
| T2 Precise on numbers, transparent on risk | Exact figures, never rounded up; risk next to the decision | «نسبة العائد المتوقع 8.5%، تصنيف المخاطر: متوسط» / "Expected return 8.5%, risk rating: medium" | «عائد ممتاز» / "Excellent returns" |
| T3 Respect, not flattery | No pet names, no praise, no celebration emoji on money | «تم استلام طلبك» / "Your request has been received" | «رائع! 🎉 أنت بطل!» / "Awesome! 🎉 You're a champion!" |
| T4 Sharia disclosures are first-class copy | 2 to 4 short sentences, readable size, explains the structure | «تستثمر صفقة في عقود مرابحة متوافقة مع أحكام الشريعة الإسلامية، تحت إشراف هيئة شرعية مستقلة. الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر، وقد تخسر جزءاً من رأس المال.» | A wall of legal text at caption size |
| T5 Errors explain what to do | The next action, not the error name | «رقم الجوال يجب أن يبدأ بـ 05» / "Phone number must start with 05" | «خطأ: قيمة غير صالحة» / "Error: invalid value" |
| T6 No fake urgency | Timers and counts are real and stated neutrally | «الصفقة تغلق خلال 3 أيام» / "Deal closes in 3 days" (when true) | «اشترك الآن!! العرض ينتهي قريباً!!!» / "Sign up now!! Offer ending soon!!!" |

## Tone by moment
The voice never changes; the tone adjusts to what the user is feeling.

| Moment | User feels | Tone | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First open, onboarding | Curious, cautious | Concrete value, one number | «استثمر في عقود مرابحة بدءاً من 1,000 ر.س» | "Invest in Murabaha contracts from 1,000 SAR" |
| KYC steps | Impatient, wary of data | Short, purposeful, say why | «نطلب بيانات دخلك لتحديد تصنيفك الاستثماري وحدودك.» | "We ask for your income to set your investor classification and limits." |
| Browsing deals | Evaluating | Factual, balanced | «نسبة العائد المتوقع 12.0% · المدة 12 شهر · تصنيف المخاطر: متوسط» | "Expected return 12.0% · Term 12 months · Risk: medium" |
| Entering an amount | Focused | Rules as hints, not warnings | «بمضاعفات 1,000 ر.س، حتى 500,000 ر.س للدفعة» | "In multiples of 1,000 SAR, up to 500,000 SAR per payment" |
| Confirming money | Careful | Name the commitment, fees, risk | «تأكيد الاستثمار» + «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر، وقد تخسر جزءاً من رأس المال.» | "Confirm investment" + "Investing carries risk; you may lose part of your capital." |
| Success | Relieved | Confirm, name, next step; no celebration | «تم استثمار 5,000.00 ر.س في الحجاز ريفيرا» | "5,000.00 SAR invested in Hijaz Riviera" |
| Payout received | Satisfied | Plain fact with amount and deal | «أُودع 1,240.00 ر.س من الريم 46 في محفظتك» | "1,240.00 SAR from Al Reem 46 was added to your wallet" |
| Late payment | Worried | Calm, what we are doing | «السداد متأخر. نتابع مع المطور.» | "Payment is late. We're following up with the developer." |
| Defaulted | Anxious | Direct, factual, next step | «الصفقة متعثرة. بدأت إجراءات التحصيل.» | "This deal has defaulted. Recovery has started." |
| Rejection (KYC, bank) | Frustrated | The specific item and the fix | «مطلوب تحديث شهادة الآيبان» | "Please update your IBAN certificate" |
| Security event | Alarmed | Factual, one clear action | «تم تسجيل الدخول من جهاز جديد. إن لم يكن أنت، غيّر رمز الدخول.» | "New device sign-in. If this wasn't you, change your passcode." |
| Empty state | Unsure if broken | What the state is + what happens next | «لا توجد صفقات مفتوحة حالياً. سنخبرك عند توفر صفقة جديدة.» | "No open deals right now. We'll notify you when a new deal is available." |
| Cash-out | Wants their money | Easy, no guilt, timeline | «سيصل المبلغ إلى حسابك خلال 1-2 يوم عمل» | "The amount reaches your bank in 1-2 business days" |

## Sentence patterns that work
- Fact + next step: «انتهت صلاحية الرمز. اطلب رمزاً جديداً.»
- Result + name: «تم حفظ الحساب البنكي»
- Rule as a hint: «يجب أن يكون الحساب باسمك كما في الهوية»
- Status + who acts: «طلبك قيد المراجعة. نرد خلال يومي عمل.»
- Choice with equal weight: «السماح» / «ليس الآن» (never confirmshaming like «لا، لا أريد معرفة أرباحي»)

## Words that set the wrong tone
| Avoid | Why | Use |
|---|---|---|
| «عذراً»، «للأسف»، "Oops", "Sorry" | Apology filler delays the fix | Start with the fact |
| «رائع!»، «مبروك!»، "Awesome!", "Congrats!" | Celebration on a financial event | «تم ...» + the thing |
| «فرصة لا تعوض»، «لا تفوّت»، "Don't miss out" | Fake urgency | The real date or amount |
| «حصري لك»، «مناسبة لك»، "Recommended for you" | Suitability claim | Behavioural reason or nothing |
| «ببساطة»، «بسهولة»، "Simply", "Just" | Condescending when the user is stuck | Remove the word |
| «تحذير!»، "Warning!" as a title | Alarm on non-critical states | The specific state |
| «اضغط هنا»، "Click here" | Says nothing about the result | The verb of the result |
| «صديقي»، "Hey buddy" | Over-familiar | No address term; or «مرحباً» + name where the pattern greets |
| Exclamation marks | Pressure or excitement | Full stop or nothing |

## Edge cases
- **Greetings**: the `PortfolioHeader` and app bar may greet by first name («مرحباً، عبدالله» / "Hello, Abdullah"). One greeting per screen, no praise attached.
- **Humour**: never in product copy, including empty states and 404s.
- **Marketing copy** can carry a hook, but still no guarantees, no fake scarcity, and past performance only with source, period and «الأداء السابق لا يعد بالضرورة مؤشراً على الأداء المستقبلي».
- **Back-office copy** is terser and may use internal status names (for example `Reimbursed`); never leak internal names to investor surfaces.
